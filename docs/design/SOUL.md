# The soul of the site

Read this before any design or UI copy in Phase 8. `DESIGN.md` says how the site looks. This file says what it must feel like, and why. When the two seem to disagree, this file wins, and you ask the product owner.

## In one line

> **Sitting across the table from someone who has seen a great deal, and is calm because of it.**
>
> مجلسٌ هادئ مع رجلٍ رأى الكثير، فصار هادئاً.

A visitor should leave feeling they **met Ahmed**, not that they browsed a consultant's website.

## What the visitor feels

| Moment | They feel | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First 5 seconds | Calm. Trust that is earned, not claimed. | Impressed, sold to, rushed |
| Reading | Spoken to by a person, in his own words | Marketed at by a brand |
| Looking for proof | Quietly reassured: the evidence is there when they want it | Shouted at by counters and logos |
| Deciding to get in touch | Invited into a conversation | Pushed into a funnel |

**The test for every page:** would a minister and a displaced mother both feel respected here? If one of them would feel talked down to, sold to, or pitied, the page is wrong.

## Five principles, and what each means on screen

### 1. He speaks; he doesn't sell

The site is in Ahmed's voice, first person. The home page opens with something he would actually say, not a job title and two buttons.

- Copy is first person singular: "أعمل مع…", "I work with…". Never "Ahmed is a leading…".
- No superlatives or hype: no "الأفضل", "الرائد", "world-class", "transforming".
- His words appear as **moments** across the site: a short letter on the home page, one sentence of his at the top of each main page, a line about why he took each project on.
- A real handwritten signature (scanned, from Ahmed) under the letter. Not a script font pretending to be one.

### 2. Quiet confidence

Someone who has nothing to prove doesn't raise their voice.

- Few things on each screen. Generous space. A slow reading rhythm.
- One accent color, used rarely, so it means something when it appears.
- Numbers are evidence in context ("twelve years with displaced communities in…"), not a loud strip of counters. The quick stats in `project.md` §6.2 stay, set quietly; whether they animate at all is Ahmed's call when he picks a direction.
- Hierarchy comes from type and space, not from boxes, borders and shadows.

### 3. Evidence, not claims

Trust is earned by showing, gently.

- Projects read as short stories: the situation, what he did, what changed. Not cards with a tag.
- Organizations, documents and certificates are present and easy to reach, set calmly, never as a logo wall at the top of the home page.
- Every claim on the page can be traced to a project, a document or a person.

### 4. Arabic is the original voice

Arabic is the language he thinks in. English is a careful translation of the same voice, not a mirror of the same layout.

- Design the Arabic page first. Arabic headings get their own scale and line height; they are not English sizes with Arabic glyphs.
- Numbers, dates and mixed-direction text are checked by eye in both languages.
- English copy is written, not machine-translated: same meaning, natural English rhythm.

### 5. Patience

Calm is also a matter of time.

- Motion is slow and rare: at most one orchestrated moment per page, like a page settling. Nothing bounces, nothing loops, nothing chases the cursor.
- Pages load fast and feel still. Respect "reduce motion" completely.

## Universal, not local

No single country's motifs, flags or landmarks. The site serves governments, international organizations and communities across many places. Heritage shows through the **Arabic typography** and the **dignity of the people in the photographs**, nothing else.

## People in photographs

- People are shown with dignity: working, talking, listening, deciding. Never as objects of pity. No staged misery, no children used for emotion.
- Field, workshop and meeting photos in natural light. Documentary, not stock.
- AI-generated images are allowed **only as dev placeholders** (see `images/README.md`). Before launch, every image of Ahmed or his work is a real photo, with consent from the people in it.

## Voice, by example

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| "Ahmed Alzubairi is a leading expert in governance." | "I help institutions make decisions they can defend, to their people and to themselves." |
| "Our comprehensive services" | "What I can help with" |
| "Trusted by 50+ organizations" | A short line under a real project: "With an international organization in…, over two years." |
| "Request a consultation now!" | "Tell me what you're facing." (button text stays from `messages/`; proposals to change it go to the product owner) |

These lines are examples of tone, not approved copy. Real copy comes from Ahmed (`project.md` §14 decision 6).

## What breaks the soul

Each of these is banned. `DESIGN.md` repeats them for the detector.

- A centered hero with a title, a subtitle and two buttons, or a photo framed in a gold border.
- The same card grid on every section. Icons in circles. A short accent bar over every heading.
- Bouncy, fast or looping counters. Logo walls high on the home page. "Trusted by" strips.
- Gradient blobs, glass effects, glow shadows, fade-and-slide on every block.
- The two "premium" clichés: **cream background + serif + warm clay or gold accent**, and **navy + gold "executive consultant"**. The current site is both. Don't come back to them.
- One word of a headline in a different color or italic. All-caps labels over every section.
- Stock photos of strangers shaking hands. AI-generated faces after launch.
